    This is a teaching model, not a vendor's machine: pressures, temperatures, forces and times are representative of a passenger-tire press with steam and nitrogen cure, and the dynamics are first-order approximations with sensor noise. Injected anomalies act on the plant or a sensor inside the model, never on a published value, so their signatures travel through the PLC the way real ones would. Shaded bands in the charts mark when one was active. One drawing unit is about 9 mm.
