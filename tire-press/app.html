Tire curing press

A green tire goes in soft and comes out vulcanised. A small PLC program runs the whole cycle below: it reads limit switches and transmitters, switches valves and cylinders, and never moves on until the machine reports that the last move is done. Everything you see is one model, so the drawing, the lamps and the logged data always agree.
